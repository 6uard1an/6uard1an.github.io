Delete an Amudi account and its data

Amudi accounts can be deleted inside the app or by an external request.

Delete immediately in the app

  1. Open Amudi and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete account, confirm, and reauthenticate.

This deletes the account’s synchronized Firestore reading data and Firebase Authentication account. Device-only books, notes, and settings remain until you select Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete personal data, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it.

Request deletion without the app

Email the address below from the email used for your Amudi account. Use the subject “Amudi account deletion request” and state that you want the account and associated cloud data deleted. Never send your password.

Email a deletion request

Amudi may ask you to verify control of the account email. Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days. Account credentials and synchronized bookmarks, notes, progress, settings, source sheets, profile fields, and related timestamps are deleted. Limited security or legal records, if any must be retained, are isolated and removed when the applicable requirement ends.
