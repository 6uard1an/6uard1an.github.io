Delete an Amudi account and its data

Amudi accounts can be deleted inside the app or by an external request.

Delete immediately in the app

  1. Open Amudi and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete account, confirm, and reauthenticate.

This deletes the account’s synchronized Firestore reading data, stored Amudi book-ownership entitlements, and Firebase Authentication account. Device-only books, notes, and settings remain until you select Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete personal data, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it.

Request deletion without the app

Email the address below from the email used for your Amudi account. Use the subject “Amudi account deletion request” and state that you want the account and associated cloud data deleted. Never send your password.

Email a deletion request

Amudi may ask you to verify control of the account email. Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days. Account credentials, synchronized bookmarks, notes, progress, settings, source sheets, profile fields, Amudi book-ownership entitlements, and related timestamps are deleted. Google Play may retain purchase transaction records under Google’s own legal and accounting obligations. Limited Amudi security or legal records, if any must be retained, are isolated and removed when the applicable requirement ends.

Delete data without deleting your account

To remove device-only reading data, open Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete personal data. To request removal of some or all synchronized bookmarks, notes, reading progress, settings, or source sheets while keeping your account, email us from your account address. Name the data you want deleted and say that you want to keep the account. Do not send your password.

Request data-only deletion

We may ask you to verify control of the account email. Verified data-only requests are normally completed within 30 days. Book ownership and account credentials remain unless you also request account deletion. Data stored only on your device must be removed there; Google Play transaction records and limited fraud-prevention records may be retained as described above.
